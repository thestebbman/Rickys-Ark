IV. LEGAL STANDARD

Under C.R.S. §14‑10‑129(4), emergency relief is required when:

1. A child is in imminent physical or emotional danger, or  
2. A parenting order is based on incorrect or incomplete evidence, or  
3. A court has failed to consider required evidence, or  
4. A parent has interfered with the parent‑child relationship.

All four apply here.
