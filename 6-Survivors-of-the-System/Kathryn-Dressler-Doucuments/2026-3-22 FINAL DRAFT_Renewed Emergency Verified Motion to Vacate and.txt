EXHIBIT 1-B (28 U.S.C. § 1738A (Parental Kidnapping Prevention Act) Definition)

SUMMARY:
28 U.S.C. § 1738A (Parental Kidnapping Prevention Act) governs interstate enforcement of child custody orders and expressly conditions full faith and credit on a court having lawful jurisdiction and issuing a custody determination consistent with statutory requirements. 


The statute prohibits interstate enforcement or modification of custody orders absent jurisdiction, written findings, and proper notice, directly contradicting enforcement of the Broward “child pick-up order” without any written UCCJEA or PKPA determination.

EXHIBIT 1-C (28 U.S.C. § 1738A(b)(4) “Home State” definition)

SUMMARY:
28 U.S.C. § 1738A(b)(4) defines “home State” as the state where the child lived with a parent (or person acting as a parent) for at least six consecutive months immediately preceding the time involved.


This definition is critical because interstate enforcement under PKPA depends on whether the enforcing court had lawful “home state” jurisdiction at the time the custody proceeding was commenced.


LEGAL ARGUMENT:
This exhibit provides the operative federal definition used to determine whether Florida could lawfully assert home-state jurisdiction when Georgia residency had accrued and was already documented.
