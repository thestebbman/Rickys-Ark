## YOUTUBE 24/7 LIVE STREAMS — WHAT YOU NEED TO KNOW

**Yes, YouTube allows 24/7 live streams.** Many channels do it. No subscriber requirement to stream from a computer using software (OBS Studio is free and handles continuous streaming with auto-reconnect if connection drops). Mobile app streams have a 12-hour limit but software streams do not.

**What you need:**
- OBS Studio (free, open source) — handles encoding and streaming
- A YouTube channel in good standing
- Stable internet upload speed (minimum 5-10 Mbps for decent video quality; 25+ Mbps for HD)
- A dedicated "stream key" for each channel

**Stream ideas from day one:**
- Pond camera (frogs, birds, critters — 24/7)
- Window camera (street, sky, birds, ambient neighborhood life)
- Coding sessions (Ricky + AI building the system live)
- Microscope feed (pond water samples, soil samples — when active)
- Nigeria node feed (Marvel and Emmanuel working — when they're on)

**If YouTube becomes restrictive:** Twitch has fewer automated-content restrictions. Peertube (open source, federated) is the fully independent option and aligns with the project's principles — no corporation owns the platform.

## LEGAL FLAGS FOR SOMTO

*These are questions, not answers. Somto leads on all of these.*

**Shipping/Import:**
- US Export Administration Regulations: Electronics exported to Nigeria generally don't require a license unless they contain controlled technology (standard consumer/professional AI hardware does not — but worth confirming for the specific GPU models)
- Nigerian Customs: Import duties on electronics. Marvel and Emmanuel should be aware of what the duty rate is on laptops, GPUs, and accessories to avoid surprise fees on arrival
- Lithium battery shipping: Airlines restrict lithium batteries in cargo. EcoFlow units should be sourced locally in Nigeria rather than shipped to avoid this complication

**YouTube/Streaming:**
- YouTube Terms of Service: Review section on "automated" content. 24/7 nature/ambient streams are common and permitted. The key is that the channel is human-managed and not violating copyright with music
- GDPR/Privacy: If the pond camera or window camera captures identifiable people (neighbors, passersby), review what disclosure is required in Massachusetts. A clear "This area is under live streaming" notice on the property is standard practice

**Entity Formation (for receiving funds and equipment):**
- Before any crowdfunding launches, a legal entity needs to exist that can receive funds and own equipment
- A Massachusetts 501(c)(3) nonprofit is the most appropriate structure for the US side
- A registered Nigerian NGO or cooperative for the Nigerian side
- Both entities need bank accounts before crowdfunding goes live
- This is the most urgent legal task — everything else can proceed while this is being established, but crowdfunding cannot launch without it

**International Transfer:**
- Sending money to Nigeria: Wire transfer, Wise (formerly TransferWise), or WorldRemit are standard. Western Union works but has high fees. Payoneer is commonly used by Nigerian freelancers and has good Nigerian bank integration
- Document all transfers in the Memory Ark from day one
