## What Is Known

Brian Blackburn was Ricky's defense attorney in 1999. He talked Ricky into accepting a Continuance Without a Finding (CWOF) — a disposition Ricky documents as a mistake and as Blackburn acting against Ricky's interests.

A CWOF in Massachusetts is not a conviction but results in a finding of sufficient facts. Ricky's position is that Blackburn steered him into this outcome rather than fighting the case, and that this decision had lasting consequences.
