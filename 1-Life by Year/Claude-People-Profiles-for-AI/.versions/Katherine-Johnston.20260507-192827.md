## Timeline of Actions

### 2013-05-01 — Trial Day
ADA Johnston (along with ADA Edward Kivari) denied Ricky access to car insurance information and phone records that he needed for his defense. Ricky was found guilty despite evidence that a car had struck him. The judge (Judge Hadley) "lied about jury being sent home by accident."

### 2013-05-10 — Evidence Submitted
ADA Johnston submitted what Ricky documents as a fake/altered receipt. Ricky did not receive a copy, despite Judge Hadley stating at the previous court date that Ricky was owed a copy.

### 2014-06-03 — ADA Claims to Find Forged Document
ADA Johnston claimed to discover a forged document and charged Ricky based on it. Ricky's note: *"She must be the greatest ada/detective, seeing the courts never checked to see who created it, they just took her word for it and charged me."*

### 2014-06-13 — Original Receipt Obtained
Ricky obtained the original, unaltered receipt. Staff provided it to him. This contradicts the altered version submitted by ADA Johnston. Ricky also obtained unaltered R1 and R5 reports from State Police.
