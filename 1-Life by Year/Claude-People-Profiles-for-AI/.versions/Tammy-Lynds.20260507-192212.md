## What Is Known

Tammy went missing on July 22, 1994 — the summer before Ricky's senior year at Putnam High School. She was approximately 15–16 years old at the time, consistent with Ricky's peer group (Ricky was born 1978, making him 16 that summer).

She died sometime between July and November 1994. Ricky did not find out the details until the funeral. Her death was not sudden in the way an accident is sudden — there was a period of not knowing what happened to her.

Ricky attended her funeral on November 17, 1994 with his mother and a person named Ellie. The next day, November 18, the day she was buried, he went alone to see Star Trek: Generations.

---

## Why She Matters

Tammy's death is listed in the life timeline without explanation or elaboration — a single line that carries enormous weight. She appears again only in the funeral entries. The brevity is the story.

She is the first documented death of a peer in Ricky's life. She died when he was 16, in the summer, before the school year started, and she wasn't buried until November. That is a long time to carry something.

The fact that Ricky went to see a movie alone the day she was buried is not escapism. It is the behavior of someone who did not have anywhere to put his grief and found the only place he could be alone with it.
